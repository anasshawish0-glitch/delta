Download Arduino sketch

The Arduino sketch expects two PCA9685 boards: 0x40 for the right legs and 0x41 for the left. If yours are wired differently, edit the CHANNEL list at the top of the sketch.

Servos need their own 5–6 V supply with a few amps on the PCA9685 V+ terminal, not the Arduino 5 V pin. Settings are saved in this browser.
