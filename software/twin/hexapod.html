Download Arduino sketch

The front is the side between legs 1 and 6. The sketch already has your channel map (board 1 = 0x40, board 2 = 0x41); for servos plugged straight into an Arduino Mega set USE_PCA9685 to 0 and fill in the pin list.

Servos need their own 5–6 V supply with a few amps on the PCA9685 V+ terminal, not the Arduino 5 V pin. Settings are saved in this browser.
